## Full description

Kabab Hut Catering helps you plan South Asian catering for events of all sizes.

With the app, you can:

- Browse menu items with categories
- Explore pre-designed catering packages
- Add menu items and packages to cart
- Enter event details like date, guest count, and notes
- Submit catering requests directly to Kabab Hut
- Save and update your profile details for faster checkout

Whether you are planning a family gathering, corporate event, or wedding function, Kabab Hut Catering gives you a simple way to submit your order details and get contacted by our team.

Why customers use this app:

- Easy, mobile-friendly ordering flow
- Authentic Pakistani and Indian catering options
- Clear package and menu browsing
- Direct contact and follow-up from Kabab Hut
