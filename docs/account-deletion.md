# Account Deletion Instructions (Kabab Hut Catering App)

Last updated: April 15, 2026

## How to request deletion

You can request account deletion in any of these ways:

1. In app:
- Go to `Profile`
- Tap `Open Account Deletion Page` or `Request Deletion by Email`

2. Email request:
- Send to: `kababhutatlanta@gmail.com`
- Subject: `Account Deletion Request - Kabab Hut Catering`
- Include your app account email and phone number

## What gets deleted

After verification, we delete or anonymize:
- Account profile details
- Personal contact details linked to the account
- Data that is not required for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, or dispute handling

## What may be retained

We may retain limited records where required for:
- Legal obligations
- Accounting/tax compliance
- Fraud/security investigations
- Dispute resolution

## Processing time

Typical processing time: up to 14 business days after identity verification.
